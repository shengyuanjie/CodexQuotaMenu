## 中文

### 本版本改进

- 优化 iPhone 锁屏顶部单行小组件，减少余量和倒计时较长时出现截断的情况，保持 13pt 字号。
- 正常显示改为 `晌99·22分  周99·22时`：省略百分号，两组之间保留两个空格，便于区分 5 小时和每周余量。
- 剩余 100% 简写为“满”，例如 `晌满·22分  周满·22时`；到达重置时间但尚未更新时简写为“待”；缺失数据仍显示 `--`。
- 保留原有规则：未来 48 小时额外重置概率达到 80% 时显示 `冲冲冲～使劲蹬啊～`，低于 80% 自动恢复余量摘要。
- Mac 菜单栏、矩形小组件、手动刷新提示和数据读取逻辑保持不变。
- 补充 99%、100%、过期和缺失数据的脚本回归测试，并更新手机配置说明。

### 更新方式

- 手机用户：下载本页附件 **CodexQuotaWidget.js**，替换 Scriptable 中现有的同名脚本内容，保留脚本名称；不要删除原脚本或创建重复副本。然后运行一次脚本或点击锁屏小组件刷新。仅更新 Mac 应用不会自动替换手机脚本。
- Mac 用户：继续提供 Apple Silicon（arm64）和 Intel（x86_64）两套安装包及 SHA-256 校验文件。本次 Mac 功能无变化，只需要手机显示优化的用户可仅更新脚本。
- 已在 iPhone 13 上确认显示效果；其他设备、日期长度和系统显示设置可能影响可用空间。后台刷新及锁屏重绘时间仍由 iOS 决定。
- 应用仍使用 ad-hoc 签名，未经过 Apple 公证。

## English

### Improvements

- Shortened the iPhone Lock Screen inline widget to reduce truncation when quota values and countdowns are long, while retaining the 13pt font.
- The normal summary is now `晌99·22分  周99·22时`. Percent signs are omitted and two spaces separate the 5-hour and weekly groups.
- A full 100% quota is abbreviated as `满`; an elapsed reset time awaiting updated data is shown as `待`. Missing data remains `--`.
- The existing 80% reset-probability rule is unchanged: `冲冲冲～使劲蹬啊～` appears at or above 80%, and the quota summary returns below 80%.
- The Mac menu bar, rectangular widget, manual-refresh messages, and data-reading logic are unchanged.
- Added script regression coverage for 99%, 100%, elapsed reset times, and missing data, and updated the mobile guide.

### Updating

- For iPhone: download **CodexQuotaWidget.js** from this release and replace the contents of the existing same-named script in Scriptable. Keep its name; do not delete it or create a duplicate. Run it once or tap the Lock Screen widget to request a refresh. Updating the Mac app alone does not update the phone script.
- Both Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Mac archives and their SHA-256 files are provided. Mac functionality is unchanged, so users who only need the phone display improvement can update the script alone.
- The display has been confirmed on an iPhone 13. Other devices, date lengths, and system display settings may affect the available space. iOS still controls background refresh and Lock Screen redraw timing.
- The app remains ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple.
