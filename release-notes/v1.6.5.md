## 中文

### 本版本改进

- Mac 菜单栏和 iPhone 锁屏小组件共用同一套重置完成检测。
- 未来 48 小时额外重置概率达到 80% 时，仍显示 `冲冲冲～使劲蹬啊～`。
- 当两组余量回升且重置时间同时进入下一周期，或两组从非满额恢复到满额时，判定本轮重置完成并立即恢复正常余量显示。
- 本轮提示一旦取消，即使预测短时间仍不低于 80% 也不会再次出现；预测先降到 80% 以下、以后再次升高时才开启新一轮提示。
- 检测状态仅保存在 Mac 本机 `UserDefaults`，不上传任务、对话、账号信息或访问令牌。
- 手机接口继续使用兼容的 `schemaVersion: 2`；新版脚本读取 Mac 的统一结果，旧版 Mac 未提供新字段时仍按原有 80% 规则显示。

## English

### Improvements

- The Mac menu bar and iPhone Lock Screen widget now share one reset-completion decision.
- The encouragement message still appears when the next-48-hour bonus-reset probability reaches 80%.
- The app treats a reset as complete when both quota summaries refill while both reset times move into their next cycles, or when both quotas refill from below full to 100%.
- Once dismissed for the current high-probability cycle, the message stays dismissed even if the forecast remains at or above 80%. It is rearmed only after the forecast first drops below 80% and later rises again.
- Detection state is stored only in local Mac `UserDefaults`; no task, conversation, account, or access-token data is uploaded.
- The phone feed remains compatible with `schemaVersion: 2`. The new script reads the Mac's unified decision, while older Mac builds without the optional field retain the previous 80% fallback.
