## 中文

### 本版本改进

- 全局额外重置预测简化为 Codex Reset Monitor 单一来源，只显示未来48小时概率。
- 手机只读接口升级为 schema v2；旧版 Scriptable 脚本会明确提示版本不匹配。
- 新增锁屏顶部单行样式：`剩82% 余6天 刷23%`。
- Scriptable 建议最早每5分钟刷新，并提供点按实时刷新和最近3天后台刷新统计；实际后台调度仍由 iOS 决定。
- 预测15分钟后标记为缓存，超过2小时隐藏旧值。
- 加固手机接口和预测读取：限制并发连接、请求超时任务、预测响应体和预测状态字段长度。

## English

### Improvements

- Simplifies the global extra-reset forecast to the single Codex Reset Monitor source and displays only the next-48-hour probability.
- Upgrades the read-only phone feed to schema v2; older Scriptable scripts now report an explicit incompatibility.
- Adds a compact inline lock-screen layout: `剩82% 余6天 刷23%`.
- Suggests the earliest Scriptable refresh after five minutes and adds tap-to-refresh plus three-day background refresh statistics; actual background scheduling remains controlled by iOS.
- Marks forecasts as cached after 15 minutes and hides them after two hours.
- Hardens the phone feed and forecast loader with concurrent-connection, timeout-work, response-size, and field-length limits.
