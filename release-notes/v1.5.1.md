## 中文

### 本版本改进

- 移除菜单栏中 `Codex` 前面的仪表盘图标，改为更简洁的纯文字显示。
- 保留剩余用量、重置倒计时、`▶` 正在执行数量和 `⏸` 等待操作数量的完整信息。
- 应用程序图标、下拉菜单、中英文界面和语言切换功能保持不变。

### 下载与安装

- 同时提供 Apple Silicon（`arm64`）和 Intel（`x86_64`）原生安装包，请按 Mac 处理器选择。
- 两套安装包均经过自动测试、发布审计，并附带独立的 SHA-256 校验文件。
- 应用启用 Hardened Runtime 并采用 ad-hoc 签名，但因项目没有 Apple Developer 证书而未经过 Apple 公证。首次启动时，请确认文件来自本仓库并通过校验，然后在 Finder 中右键应用选择“打开”。

## English

### Improvements

- Removed the gauge icon before `Codex` in the menu bar for a cleaner text-only presentation.
- Preserved the full remaining-usage percentage, reset countdown, `▶` running count, and `⏸` waiting count.
- The application icon, drop-down menu, bilingual interface, and language switching remain unchanged.

### Downloads and installation

- Native builds are included for both Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x86_64`). Choose the archive matching your Mac.
- Both builds are tested, release-audited, and accompanied by separate SHA-256 checksum files.
- The app uses Hardened Runtime and an ad-hoc signature, but is not notarized because the project has no Apple Developer certificate. After verifying that the download came from this repository, you may need to right-click the app in Finder and choose **Open** on first launch.
