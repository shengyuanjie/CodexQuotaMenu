## English

### New Features

- Added the primary 24-hour global bonus-reset probability to the menu bar, with 48-hour probability, confidence, freshness, and update time in the menu.
- Added a separate fast `⚡ Tibo` signal. It is never averaged into the primary probability and is suppressed after a recent reset to reduce false alerts.
- Added a default-off, Bearer-token-protected local read-only feed and an importable Scriptable accessory rectangular lock-screen widget.

### Privacy and Reliability

- The two public forecast sources refresh and fail independently without blocking personal quota or task status.
- The primary cache contains public data only and is hidden after two hours. Phone responses exclude task titles, paths, conversations, and Codex credentials.
- The phone token is stored in macOS Keychain and in Scriptable Keychain on iPhone.
