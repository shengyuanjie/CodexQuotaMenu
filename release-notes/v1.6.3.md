## 中文

> 状态：正式发布。

### 本版本改进

- 菜单栏常驻标题显示 5 小时与每周用量、各自的重置剩余时间和正在执行的任务数量，并加宽信息组之间的空白间距。
- 当最近 48 小时重置率达到 80% 时，用 `冲冲冲～使劲蹬啊～` 暂时代替两组用量信息；低于 80% 后自动恢复。
- 菜单栏不再直接显示重置率，下拉菜单继续保留重置预测详情。
- 常驻标题中的重置剩余时间向下取整到天、小时或分钟；下拉菜单继续保留精确时间。
- iPhone 顶部单行小组件改为 `晌81%·3时  周62%·2天`；重置率达到 80% 时切换为 `冲冲冲～使劲蹬啊～`，低于 80% 后自动恢复。

## English

> Status: Released.

### Improvements

- The menu-bar title now shows both 5-hour and weekly quotas, their reset countdowns, and the active-task count, with wider spacing between groups.
- When the next-48-hour reset probability reaches 80%, `Go go go~ Pedal harder~` temporarily replaces both quota groups and switches back below 80%.
- The reset probability is no longer shown directly in the title; its details remain available in the menu.
- Reset countdowns in the title are floored to days, hours, or minutes, while the menu keeps the detailed time.
- The iPhone inline widget now shows `晌81%·3时  周62%·2天` and switches to `冲冲冲～使劲蹬啊～` at an 80% or higher reset probability, restoring the quota summary below 80%.
